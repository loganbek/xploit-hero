# XPLOIT HERO

An idle/merge cyber RPG that runs entirely in one HTML file. You play as Muse,
a hacker with a netbook, clearing sectors of daemons, merging loot into
stronger gear, and jacking into sector mainframes to run real terminal
commands.

## ▶️ Play now — one tap

[Play XPLOIT HERO](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/loganbek/xploit-hero@main/index.html)

Opens the latest build right in your browser — no install, no account.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser (phone or desktop). No install, no
account, no server. Your progress saves in the browser's local storage.

- **Move:** drag anywhere (touch) or WASD / arrow keys. Your rig auto-fires
  at the nearest daemon. No walls: the world scrolls as you roam, and the
  camera follows your rig.
- **INJECT NOVA:** burst attack — button, or SPACE on a keyboard. 10s cooldown.
- **PATCH UP:** heal — button, or H on a keyboard. 12s cooldown.
- **Loot:** walk over glowing drops. Gear auto-merges (two of a tier make the
  next tier up). Crates push, barrels smash, data nodes crack open gear.
- **Clear a sector:** kill every daemon in the wave. Boss every 10 stages.
- **Mainframes:** on stages ending in 5, clear the wave, then walk to the
  server, read FUSE's briefing, and jack in. The terminal teaches real
  commands (`ls`, `cat`, `chmod +x`, `nmap`, `ssh`) inside the story.

## Versions

`index.html` is always the latest build. Version history lives in the
`xploit-hero-poc-vN.html` files in the project workspace.
